Life of a Tyrannosaur

Tyrannosaurus Rex is the most iconic and well known prehistoric animal of all time. it was described back 1905 and originally giving a different name "Manospondylus Gigus" but kept the name T.Rex due to it's popularity.

Tyrannosaurus lived in the late Cretaceus 67-66 million years ago at the end of the mesozoic. in life it had a stocky, robust build with a large bulky tail to balance itself. it also is well known for having small, two fingered arms. tyrannosaurs evolved smaller arms because they would use their mouths more for hunting, making the arms become smaller overtime.

Tyrannosaurus lived with other iconic animals in it's environment such as Triceratops, Ankylosaurus, Edmontosaurus and Azhdarchid Pterasaurs. it's dite would mostly consist of animals like Triceratops and Edmontosaurus with actual evidence of Predation on fossils of these animals. Tyrannosaurus had a wide range across north america with remains spanning form Canada to the US.
